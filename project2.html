Cinematic drone build — avionics & electrical systems

Component selection, assembly, and flight-control configuration for a 7-inch racing drone

Electrical systems Soldering & assembly Betaflight

What it did

Contributed to the avionics of a 7-inch first-person-view cinematic drone as part of a student build team — selecting electrical components, assembling and soldering the power/control system, and configuring flight software.

💭 Design reasoning

  • Selected the motor and battery combination by matching current draw, voltage, and KV rating — using a lower-KV motor with a higher-cell-count battery to provide sufficient torque for the larger 7-inch propellers without causing excessive motor speed.
  • Hand-assembled and soldered the flight controller, ESC, and power-distribution wiring, ensuring reliable electrical connections that could withstand the vibration and mechanical demands of flight.
  • Configured Betaflight flight-controller firmware to calibrate motor response and tune flight characteristics, translating the hardware configuration into stable, controllable flight behaviour.

What changed

Took a set of individually-selected components with no guarantee of compatibility, and turned them into a working, tuned, flight-ready system — closing the loop between electrical component selection and real physical performance.

Who benefited

The build team gained a working aircraft that performed reliably in flight; the process also built direct, hands-on experience translating electrical calculations into physical hardware choices that had to actually work under real load, not just on paper.

✓ Outcome

Successfully assembled and flown as a 7-inch FPV racing drone, with flight characteristics tuned via Betaflight following assembly.
